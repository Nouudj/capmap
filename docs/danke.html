Thanks for your purchase!

Here is how to unlock CapMap in two minutes.

1. Download CapMap

Get CapMap for Windows, Mac with Apple chip or Mac with Intel.

2. Install

Windows: Open CapMap-Setup.exe. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway.

Mac: Unzip the file and drag CapMap into your Applications folder. On first launch: right-click CapMap → Open → Open. If that does not work, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3. Unlock

On first launch CapMap asks for your license key. You will find it in your purchase email. Copy, paste, click Unlock – done. Your key works on two devices; under Options → License you can sign a device out again.
